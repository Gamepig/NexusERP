# Task ID: 11
# Title: Phase 1: Basic Financials (Accounts Payable)
# Status: done
# Dependencies: 10
# Priority: medium
# Description: Implement the Accounts Payable functionality, linking purchase orders to invoices and tracking payments to suppliers, as specified in PRD 3.6.1.
# Details:
Create `invoices` (linked to POs), `accounts_payable`, `payments`, and `currencies` tables. Develop logic to generate AP entries upon PO receipt/invoicing. Implement APIs for: creating supplier invoices (`POST /api/invoices`), querying invoices (`GET /api/invoices`), recording payments (`POST /api/payments`), querying accounts payable records (`GET /api/accounts-payable`), and generating invoices directly from purchase orders (`POST /api/invoices/generate-from-po/{po_id}`). Ensure multi-currency support is fully integrated.

# Test Strategy:
Create a PO, receive it, generate a supplier invoice, and verify that an AP record is created. Record a partial payment and check that the outstanding balance is correctly updated.
